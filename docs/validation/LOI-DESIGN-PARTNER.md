# Design partner & LOI templates

Use only after Gate B. Binary asks — enthusiasm is noise ([Bessemer](https://www.bvp.com/atlas/design-partners-the-pre-launch-edge-most-ai-founders-ignore)).

## Design partner (90 days)

**We give:** early Teams policy/audit mock + free core forever for ICs; biweekly feedback slot; roadmap influence.  
**You give:** 90-day engagement; biweekly feedback; case study / logo / reference rights when comfortable; honest kill feedback.

```text
Design Partner — MCP Watchdog Teams (draft)

Company: ________
Owner: ________ (title)
Start: ________  End: ________ (+90d)

Success criteria (pick measurable):
[ ] Policy dry-run on our MCP allowlist
[ ] Audit of who approved which MCP in period
[ ] Fleet/status view useful in standup

Conversion ask at day 90: go paid Teams at $___ / ___ seats, or decline in writing.
```

## LOI (specific terms)

```text
Letter of Intent — non-binding except confidentiality

Buyer: ________
Budget owner: ________
Product: MCP Watchdog Teams (trust policy + approval audit)
Price band discussed: $___ / mo or $___ / seat
Target decision date: ________
Pilot: $[1–500] for __ days — YES / NO

Signed: ________ Date: ________
```

## Paid pilot (strongest Tier A)

Even $1 flips psychology (Capitaly / SeedForge). Prefer small paid pilot over vague LOI.
